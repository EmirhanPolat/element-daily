Six Star Uploader

A small private tool that uploads and schedules videos for one YouTube channel, Element Daily: one chemical element every day, from 1 to 118.

What it does

  • Uploads the channel owner's own videos to the channel owner's own YouTube channel.
  • Sets the title, description, tags and publishing time of those videos.
  • Reads the status of those videos to check that they were published.

Who can use it

Only the channel owner. It is not offered to other people and has no sign-up.
